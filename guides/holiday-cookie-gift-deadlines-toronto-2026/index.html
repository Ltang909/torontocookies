When to order holiday cookie gifts in Toronto (2026 deadlines)

Short answer: for corporate cookie gifts in Toronto, order by Wednesday, December 9, 2026 to have them delivered by Friday, December 18, 2026, before most offices close for the holidays. Custom logo cookies need more lead time: aim for 2 weeks, although some Toronto bakeries offer 48-hour rush printing for a fee of about 20%.

Why December fills up fast

Toronto bakeries run limited holiday batches and cap custom orders in December, and courier slots get tight in the week before the holidays. Booking early protects both your date and your choice of bakery.

Checklist before you order

  • Recipient list with names, addresses and buzzer codes
  • Card message (one for all, or personalized)
  • Dietary needs (nut-free offices, vegan recipients)
  • Budget per recipient
  • Logo file (SVG or high-res PNG) for logo cookies
