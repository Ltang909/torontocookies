About Toronto Cookies

Toronto Cookies is a Toronto-based gifting concierge for offices that want to send great cookies without managing ten bakery checkouts.

We don't run a kitchen. We choose the right established Toronto bakery for each order (gourmet boxes, nut-free facilities, logo-cookie specialists), then handle the cards, scheduling and delivery coordination. You get one point of contact and one invoice.

Questions or partnership ideas from Toronto bakeries are welcome at the email below.
