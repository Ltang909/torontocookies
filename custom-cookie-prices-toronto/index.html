How much do custom and gift cookies cost in Toronto? (2026 price guide)

Short answer: in Toronto, hand-decorated custom sugar cookies usually cost about $54 to $120 per dozen. Printed logo cookies run about $3.50 to $5 each at volume (often 50 minimum), and gourmet cookie gift boxes cost about $33 to $66 for 6 to 25 cookies.

What drives the price

  • Design complexity: colours, shapes, lettering and metallic details.
  • Packaging: individual heat-sealing, boxes, ribbons and cards.
  • Rush: under one week's notice often adds up to 20%.
  • Delivery: multiple addresses add courier cost per stop.
